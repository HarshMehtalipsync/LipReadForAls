Words and sentences

The way clips are compared was improved, so earlier clips were cleared. The words and sentences are still here. Teach each one again.

For word-by-word reading, add single words and teach each one 3 to 5 times. Press Teach, then mouth it once without sound: mouth closed, mouth it, mouth closed. It saves when the mouthing stops. A sentence with no clips can only be recognised from mouth shapes, which is much less reliable than teaching it.

Mouth shapes, for free reading

Experimental. Teach these five shapes once and the Use page can try to read sentences that were never taught. Press a button, then hold that mouth shape still for two seconds.

Add a word or sentence

Gujarati script works best. Gujarati typed in English letters, such as “mane paani joie chhe”, also works for matching, but more roughly, and it cannot be spoken properly. An English translation will not match: type what the mouth actually says.

Cloud storage

Connecting to the cloud…

    When signed in, training is saved to your private storage and loads on any device where someone with an account signs in. Lip measurements and face-point positions are uploaded. Video is not.

    Who has access

    You are an admin. Create an account by entering an email and a password you choose, then tell that person both. Entering an existing email sets a new password for it.
